About

A frontend engineer who cares about the millisecond after page load.

Some context on who I am and how I got here.

I'm Daniel Tran, 29, based in Singapore. I grew up between Saigon and Singapore, picked up coding in high school after spending too many hours bending Greasemonkey scripts to my will, and graduated from NUS with a B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Science in 2019.

My career started as a full-stack engineer at a Series-B fintech, but I drifted toward the frontend after realizing I cared more about what users actually see and feel than about another microservice. Spent a few years leading frontend at a games platform — that's where I fell down the Three.js rabbit hole and learned the hard way that "WebGL is fast" is a half-truth without a perf budget.

These days I lean pragmatic over purist. I'd rather ship a 90% solution that real users love than perfect architecture nobody touches.

What I care about

  • Performance. Core Web Vitals matter. P75, not averages. The first 200ms is where users decide if they trust your app.
  • Accessibility. It's not optional. Screen-reader testing belongs in CI, not in someone's "later" pile.
  • Developer experience. Your tooling shapes your output. Slow pnpm install is slow team. Confusing error messages are tomorrow's bugs.
  • Boring code. Clever code is a liability. The best PR I ever wrote deleted 200 lines and added zero.
