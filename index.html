About

I grew up between Saigon and Singapore, picked up coding in high school, and graduated from NUS with a B.Sc. in Computer Science (Hons) in 2019.

My career started full-stack at a Series-B fintech, but I drifted toward the frontend after realizing I cared more about what users actually see and feel than about another microservice. Spent a few years leading frontend at a games platform — that's where I picked up Three.js and learned how to make WebGL fast.

These days I lean pragmatic over purist. I'd rather ship a 90% solution that real users love than perfect architecture nobody touches. Strong opinions on performance (Core Web Vitals matter), accessibility (it's not optional), and developer experience (your tooling shapes your output).

Outside work: I write occasionally on this site, contribute to a few OSS libraries, hike when the weather plays nice, and run a hobby project rendering generative art with shaders.
